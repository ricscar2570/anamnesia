# Help Shape the Future of AnamnesiA — Playtesters Wanted

**Do you remember AnamnesiA?**

Thanks to your support on Kickstarter, AnamnesiA became a real, published game. And now I’m working on something I want to approach with the same care: a thoroughly revised **Second Edition**.

This is not simply an expanded version with more pages. I’m revisiting the game from the ground up while protecting what made AnamnesiA what it is: fragmented memories, shared trauma, conflicting recollections, and a truth that emerges through play rather than being controlled by a single player.

The new edition is now entering its first **Human Alpha Playtest**, and I would love some of the original backers to be part of it.

I’m looking for small groups of **2–4 players** willing to play a complete session of the revised version of **The Incident**, using a dedicated Alpha playtest kit, and then provide structured feedback.

You do **not** need to be a game designer or an experienced playtester. In fact, I need different kinds of groups: experienced narrative gamers, traditional RPG players, people who remember the original AnamnesiA well, and people who have not played it in a long time.

A session should take approximately **90–150 minutes**, plus a short post-game questionnaire.

Some of the things I specifically want to test are:

- whether the revised rules are clearer and easier to teach;
- whether conflicting memories emerge naturally during play;
- whether players still feel genuine narrative freedom;
- whether the new way of building the final truth works without a predetermined solution;
- whether Stress, Echoes and Breakdowns create the right dramatic pressure;
- and, most importantly, whether this still **feels like AnamnesiA**.

For a few groups, I am particularly interested in a **blind playtest**: you receive the material, read it, and play without any additional explanation from me. That is one of the best ways for me to discover whether the game can truly teach itself.

If you backed AnamnesiA on Kickstarter and would like to help shape its Second Edition, **reply to this message / contact me and tell me how many players would be in your group and whether you have already played the original game**.

I’ll provide everything you need to play.

The first edition of AnamnesiA exists because you helped make it possible.

I would be very happy if some of you were also there to help determine what it becomes next.

**Thank you — and welcome back to the memory.**

**Riccardo Scaringi**  
Designer of *AnamnesiA*
